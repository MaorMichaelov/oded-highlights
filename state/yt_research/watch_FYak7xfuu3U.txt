### 4. Technical Analysis for Editor Copy-Cat (in English)

#### **Structure & Pacing:**
- **Hook (00:00 - 00:30)**: Direct intro with cold, awkward energy establishing the interview style.
- **Body (00:30 - 35:40)**: Alternating high-tension interrogation, absurd physical stunts (intruder, manager swap), and structured ad breaks.
- **Ending (35:40 - 36:36)**: Quick handshake wrap-up leading into a cliffhanger joke about the next guest.

#### **Editing & Aesthetic Rules:**
1. **Static Master Setup**: Stark white-wall background, two cheap folding tables, zero decorative set design.
2. **Reaction Cutting**: Hard cuts to close-ups immediately on line delivery to enhance deadpan anti-humor.
3. **Absurd Disruptions**: Scripted character entrances integrated as natural interruptions to keep engagement high.
4. **Polished Ad Integration**: Hard-cut away from the studio set to a centered solo frame with clean bottom-third graphics and promo codes.
5. **No Music / Raw Audio**: Reliance entirely on voice, physical room noise, and deliberate awkward silence rather than sound effects or soundtracking.
